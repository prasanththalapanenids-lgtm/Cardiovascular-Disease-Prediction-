About CardioPredict

This application predicts the likelihood of cardiovascular disease based on your health parameters using machine learning models such as XGBoost, Random Forest, AdaBoost, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and KNN.

The dataset includes features like age, gender, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose levels, smoking, alcohol consumption, and physical activity.
